WeatherFitMe privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the WeatherFitMe app for Android and the WeatherFitMe web app at naimbh.github.io/weatherfitme. WeatherFitMe is made by Naim Bin Hasan (“I”, “me”).

In short
  • There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. I do not sell or share your information.
  • Your places, settings, character and wardrobe stay on your device.
  • To show the weather, the app sends the coordinates of the places you look at to weather services.
  • Only if you turn on alerts does WeatherFitMe’s own service keep your home place and alert settings, and it deletes them when you turn alerts off.

What stays on your device

Your saved places and which one is home, your units and other settings, the look of your character, your wardrobe, recent forecasts and any weather service keys you add are kept in the app’s storage on your device. They are not sent to me. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Your location

If you allow it, the app reads your device’s location to show the weather where you are. It uses the location to ask for the forecast and the name of the place, as described below, and does not keep a history of where you have been. You can say no and add places by name instead. The app only reads your location while you are using it.

Photos

“Match my photo” lets you pick skin and hair colours for your character from a photo. The photo is used on your device only. It is never uploaded or saved.

To show the weather, maps and place names, the app connects to the services below. Like any connection on the internet, each one also sees your device’s IP address. Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Alerts and the morning briefing

Alerts are off until you turn them on.

  • In the Android app, the app asks WeatherFitMe’s own service every few hours which alerts are coming. It sends the coordinates and name of your home place, your units, your warmth and clothing style, your briefing time and your alert choices. The service works out the alerts and keeps none of this.
  • In the web app, the same settings are kept by WeatherFitMe’s own service, together with a push address that your browser creates for notifications, so that alerts can reach you while the page is closed. They are deleted when you turn alerts off, or after the push address stops working.

What WeatherFitMe does not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no name, email or phone number is asked for.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information for advertising.

Security

Every connection the app makes uses encryption (HTTPS).

Children

WeatherFitMe is a general weather app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your information

Turn alerts off to delete everything WeatherFitMe’s own service keeps about you. Uninstall the app, or clear its data, to remove everything on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
